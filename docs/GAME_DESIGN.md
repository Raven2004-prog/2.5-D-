# Game Design — Ash Witness: The Same Rain

## Product promise

A beautiful, humane fantasy investigation game where knowledge persists but relationships do not. The player learns Greyfen's systems, earns specific people's trust in the present line, survives intelligent patrols, and succeeds by sharing agency rather than optimizing people as pieces.

## Format and visual direction

- 2D top-down exploration with a 2.5D presentation.
- Painterly title art paired with hand-authored vector and procedural game art.
- Rain, fog, amber ward-light, long soft shadows, layered silhouettes, camera ease, and restrained impact effects.
- Palette: ash charcoal, rain blue, fen teal, bronze sunset, amber light, worn parchment, sparing danger red.
- Compatibility renderer and 1152 × 648 reference viewport for broad hardware support.

## Playable structure

1. **The Blink** — Earth memory, arrival beneath the split ash, language accommodation, Lysa's token, and arrest.
2. **Bells for a Massacre** — reach Greyfen, witness the three-part false flag, and experience the first Return.
3. **The Same Rain** — use retained knowledge to prove one prediction and discover why stopping one fuse is not enough.
4. **Three Hands on the Knife** — investigate the signal, water gate, and granary; hidden assumptions damage present-line trust.
5. **No One Owes Him Yesterday** — disclose facts as facts, propose risks, and let each ally decide.
6. **Six People Break the Pattern** — survive the coordinated operation while Piri, Brann, Nessa, Kesh, Lysa, and Mara own their choices.
7. **Bound for Lysford** — tribunal outcome, G-1, and a complete ending with a forward hook.

## Player verbs

- Walk, sprint, dodge, and take cover through movement.
- Talk, inspect, and choose how much to disclose.
- Use Focus to highlight noticed routes and contradictions; Focus is observation, not magic.
- Brace and shove attackers non-lethally. Evan is never a dominant fighter.
- Open the Memory Folio to distinguish retained echoes from present-line evidence.
- Build a plan by proposing contributions rather than assigning people without consent.

## State layers

### Retained across a Return

- Accommodation and learned language concepts.
- Facts, hypotheses, and route knowledge.
- Death memories, soul scars, and trauma tags.
- Authored death count and accessibility settings.

### Reset with the current line

- Physical evidence and inventory.
- NPC trust, familiarity, and accepted risks.
- Injuries, patrol positions, alarms, and local quest state.
- The clock, the courier token, and all relationships earned only in the erased line.

## AI contract

Agents use a deterministic state machine: patrol → notice → investigate → chase → strike → search → return. Sight requires both range/angle and an unobstructed ray. Sprinting creates a larger hearing event. Suspicion is telegraphed before pursuit. A non-lethal shove interrupts an attack; repeated pressure makes an agent withdraw rather than die.

Named NPCs do not wait to become inventory. They have fixed motives, authored dialogue, and explicit conditions for accepting risk. Their final contributions happen independently once accepted.

## Success and failure

Authored catastrophe beats trigger the Return. Ordinary stealth mistakes cost health, time, or position and can lead into the current authored failure; they are not an infinite mechanical resource. Final success requires evidence for all three foundations and six present-line contributions.

## Accessibility

Reduced motion, reduced flash, high contrast, scalable interface, captions, keyboard/controller input, and forgiving interaction radii are first-class settings. The story treats panic, dissociation, and self-destructive choices seriously without graphic death imagery.
