# Canon Locks

- Evan Hale is nineteen, physically ordinary, anxious, kind, observant, and untrained. He never becomes a power fantasy.
- Earth is not one of Evara's Fourfold planes. The Return comes from a foreign blessing Evan does not understand.
- Only true death triggers the Return. G-0 forms after stable embodiment and the first accommodation.
- Retained memory is imperfect. Trauma and soul scars persist; items, proof, injuries, conditioning, NPC memories, and failed-line intimacy do not.
- The false flag has three independent foundations: altered signal horns, water-gate sabotage, and powder under the refugee granary.
- Corvin remembers no erased line. He infers the recurrence from surviving evidence and a spirit lens.
- Daevar are Middle-Realm peoples, not infernal beings. “Demon” appears only as prejudiced or inaccurate speech.
- Kesh is mixed-lineage Daevar. Do not assign a single lineage unless the source canon changes.
- Mara's Ash Witness Compact is public surety, not ownership or a magical bond.
- Final victory belongs to Piri, Brann, Nessa, Kesh, Lysa, Mara, and Evan together. Each person chooses their own risk.
- Tomas can surrender. Nonviolence is a meaningful route, not missing combat content.
- The ending forms G-1 only after testimony is accepted and Greyfen returns to stable command.

When sources conflict, the character bible overrides stale names in the terminology registry. This game adapts Arc 1 and does not reveal the saga's protected late-story mysteries.
